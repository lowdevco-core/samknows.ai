Key takeaways

What is the /effects skill?★

It's a free Claude Code skill I built that adds visual effects to your videos. You drop a clip into Claude, type /effects and the name of an effect, and Claude does the edit: it looks at your footage, builds the effect around you, and saves a new video with your original audio untouched.

There are 51 effects, from a moody colour grade to reels orbiting your head and an Instagram comment that types itself. Every effect tracks your face, so anything that would cover it gets placed behind your head instead. The source is at github.com/lowdevco/Video-editing-effects.

What do you need before you start?★

You need Claude Code, either the desktop app or the terminal version, and a video. Vertical phone footage with you clearly in shot works best.

Under the hood the skill uses ffmpeg and Python. You don't have to install them yourself: on first use Claude checks for both and installs whichever is missing. That first setup takes about two minutes.

How do you install it?★

One command. Open Terminal, or the terminal inside Claude Code, and run:

git clone https://github.com/lowdevco/Video-editing-effects.git ~/.claude/skills/effects

On Windows (PowerShell), use this instead:

git clone https://github.com/lowdevco/Video-editing-effects.git $HOME\.claude\skills\effects

Restart Claude Code when it finishes.

Rather not touch the terminal? Paste this into Claude Code and it installs the skill for you:

Install the skill from github.com/lowdevco/Video-editing-effects.git into ~/.claude/skills/effects

How do you add an effect to a video?★

  1. Open Claude Code, the app or the terminal.
  2. Drag your video into the chat.
  3. Type the effect you want and when it should happen.

For example:

/effects orbit from 3 to 6 seconds
/effects spotlight when I say "kill the lights", with the word Claudit behind me
/effects comment-box at the end, typing CLAUDE
/effects glass-widgets for the first 3 seconds

Claude first checks a few frames and moves anything that would cover your face. Then it saves yourvideo_effects.mp4 next to your original, with the original audio kept.

Combine effects in one request★

You can stack several effects in one go. Just describe them in order:

Add locked-on from 6 to 10s, then orbit from 10 to 13s, then a reject stamp on 'no'.

How do you get the best results?★

  • Shoot vertical. Phone footage with you clearly visible works best.
  • Describe timing like you'd say it. “When I say ‘watch’” works. Claude finds the matching moment.
  • Use your own reels. For orbit, noise, phone-fan or profile-wall, give Claude a folder of screenshots of your reels.
  • Give real numbers. Charts, counters and headlines use the figures you provide, so give the actual ones.
  • Pick your resolution. Output is 1080p by default. You can ask for 4K, but it takes longer to render.

What can the skill not do?★

These are starter versions. They won't recreate my reels exactly, because those edits also use sound design and carefully tuned timing.

The effects don't add sound or captions either. Add those afterwards in an editor like CapCut. And treat every result as a draft: Claude makes the edit, but watch the finished video before you post it.

FAQ★

Is the /effects skill free?★

Yes. It's on GitHub and costs nothing to install. You do need Claude Code, which may need a paid Claude plan depending on your account.

Does it change my audio?★

No. The edited video keeps your original audio. The effects are visual only.

Where is the edited video saved?★

Next to your original, as yourvideo_effects.mp4. Your original file isn't changed.

Does it work on Windows?★

Yes. Use the PowerShell install command above. Claude installs ffmpeg and Python on first use if you don't have them.

Can I use more than one effect in a video?★

Yes. Describe them in one request with their timings, for example locked-on, then orbit, then a reject stamp.
