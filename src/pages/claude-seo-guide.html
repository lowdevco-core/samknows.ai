Claude SEO is a free Claude Code skill that audits a website and tells you what to fix first. Type /seo audit and a URL, and it checks technical SEO, content quality, structured data and AI search signals, then gives you a prioritised roadmap inside Claude.

Key takeaways

  • 22 /seo commands, from full audits to schema, local SEO and GEO for AI search.
  • Start with /seo audit https://yoursite.com for a prioritised roadmap.
  • Covers technical SEO, content and E-E-A-T, structured data, AI search, and local and e-commerce SEO.
  • It analyses and plans. You, or Claude Code, make the changes.

What is the Claude SEO skill?★

It's a free Claude Code skill I built for website analysis: full audits, technical SEO, content quality and AI search optimisation. Give it a URL and it checks the site's search signals, then hands you a prioritised roadmap of what to fix, right inside Claude.

Everything runs through one command, /seo, followed by what you want and the URL. The source is at github.com/lowdevco/claude-seo.

What do you need before you start?★

You need Claude Code, the desktop app or the terminal version, and the URL of a site you want to check. It can be your own site, a client's or a competitor's.

How do you install it?★

The easiest way is to let Claude do it. Paste this into Claude Code:

Install the skill from github.com/lowdevco/claude-seo.git into ~/.claude/skills/seo

Prefer the terminal? Clone it into your skills folder yourself. On Mac or Linux:

git clone https://github.com/lowdevco/claude-seo.git ~/.claude/skills/seo

On Windows (PowerShell):

git clone https://github.com/lowdevco/claude-seo.git $HOME\.claude\skills\seo

Restart Claude Code when it finishes, then type /seo to check it's there.

How do you run your first audit?★

One command:

/seo audit https://example.com

Swap in your own URL. A full audit sends parallel specialist agents through the site, each checking a different area, and pulls their findings into one prioritised list. Start at the top of that list: it's ordered so the biggest wins come first.

What does it check?★

  • Technical SEO: crawlability, indexability, redirects, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, Core Web Vitals (including INP) and hreflang.
  • Content and E-E-A-T: content depth, readability, semantic keyword clustering and structured content briefs.
  • Structured data: Schema.org JSON-LD generation and error detection.
  • AI search and GEO: how citable your pages are for AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini and ChatGPT.
  • Local and commerce: Google Business Profile optimisation, local citation signals and product markup.

Which command should you start with?★

  • New to SEO, or checking a whole site? /seo audit.
  • One page isn't ranking? /seo page on that URL.
  • Want to show up in ChatGPT and AI Overviews? /seo geo.
  • Local business? /seo local.
  • Writing a new post? /seo content-brief with your topic, before you write.

What can the skill not do?★

It analyses and plans; it doesn't change your site on its own. You get a roadmap, and you (or Claude Code, if your site's code is in front of it) make the edits.

Treat the findings like advice from a sharp consultant: check anything important before you act on it, and re-run the audit after big changes to see what moved.

FAQ★

Is the Claude SEO skill free?★

Yes. It's on GitHub and costs nothing to install. You do need Claude Code, which may need a paid Claude plan depending on your account.

What is GEO?★

Generative Engine Optimisation: making your pages easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews to find, understand and cite. /seo geo checks that for you.

Can I audit a competitor's site?★

Yes. Any public URL works. /seo competitor-pages can also generate comparison pages against them.

Will it fix my site for me?★

Not by itself. It tells you what to fix and in what order. If your site's code is open in Claude Code, you can then ask Claude to make the changes.
